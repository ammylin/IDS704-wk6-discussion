💡 Key Takeaways from the Activity

  • Overall Accuracy Illusion: A model can be 98% accurate overall while concealing 6.2% error rates on specific intersecting subgroups.
  • Technical Accuracy vs Governance: Model updates (e.g. 99.2% accuracy) do NOT solve missing non-binary representation, low-light errors, or lack of consent and appeal channels.
  • Contextual & Risk-Based Proportionality: An automated error in gym access is an inconvenience; a false match in police identification leads to severe, irreversible consequences.

TEACHING GUIDE

📘 Facilitator Guide: Classroom Activity Mapping

How the New Mechanics Surface Key Discussion Questions:

  • Evidence Unlocking (Cards 1–6): Forces students to read and interpret empirical data (e.g., aggregate 98% vs 6.2% subgroup error) rather than guessing answers.
  • Breaking Event Cards: Events like the False Match Incident and Model Update (v1 vs v2) demonstrate that technical accuracy improvements do not eliminate governance, consent, or surveillance issues.
  • Conditional Safeguards & "What Would Change Your Mind?": Teaches students to move beyond binary "Launch vs Ban" debates by designing proportional safeguards and defining evidence-based thresholds.
  • "What Changed?" Reflection: Visualizes how team opinions evolve as information grows, rewarding thoughtful reasoning over a fixed "correct" score.
